01 The company

Fandom is for everyone.

Dastanverse is a newly registered retail business based in the Netherlands, focused on stories, characters and the things that help us feel connected to them.

We’re preparing an online store for fans and thoughtful gift-givers alike. Our focus is on collectibles, licensed fandom merchandise and giftable products that make a favourite story feel a little closer.

We’re at the beginning of our journey, building our first selection and connecting with suppliers who share our attention to quality and authenticity.

02 A note from the founder

It started with Tintin & Snowy.

A childhood love of comics. A new chapter with Dastanverse.

I’m a programmer working in IT, but my love of stories began long before my career. As a child, I fell in love with Tintin and Snowy and eagerly read the comic strips at the back of magazines.

During the pandemic, that love became a bigger part of my life. I began buying and reading comics more seriously, simply because of the joy they brought me.

I’ve always been drawn to figures, too: the details, the characters, and the chance to bring a little of an imagined world into our own. Collecting them is something I love.

Dastanverse grew out of that passion. I want to share the pleasure of discovering stories and characters with other fans and with people looking for a meaningful gift. And one day, I hope to create new worlds and characters of my own.

The founder of Dastanverse
